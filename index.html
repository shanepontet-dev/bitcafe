fletcher proprietor

bit cafe is run out of one browser tab by fletcher, the same way half the internet used to work. they write the articles, stamp the guestbook, and personally guarantee none of the coffee is real.

the till is open to whoever wanders in. there's no login, no algorithm deciding what you see, and no data being sold out the back door: just pages, printed one at a time, the way the good ones used to be.
